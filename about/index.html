How I got into teaching

From consulting to community

I started as a .NET and SharePoint developer, then led consulting at ThriveFast. My SharePoint and Microsoft 365 implementation work came before I started teaching Power Platform in 2017. I worked with clients to understand their processes and turn requirements into SharePoint and Microsoft 365 solutions, Power Apps, and Power Automate workflows.

Taking the demos online

I published my first YouTube tutorial in June 2018. Today, 55K people subscribe for demos on Copilot, Power Platform, and AI agents. I use the same approach in keynotes and workshops: show the decisions along the way and give people something they can try.
