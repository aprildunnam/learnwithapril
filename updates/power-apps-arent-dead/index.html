IGNITE 2026 FOLLOW-UP · POWER APPS

Your Power Apps aren't dead. They just got a promotion.

Every Ignite headline this year was about agents, Cowork and Copilot Code, and I keep getting the same question: "Should I stop building Power Apps?" No. Your apps are getting Copilot built in, bigger data limits, and a new job as tools that agents can use. Here's everything that's new, and what to do about it.

By April Dunnam·November 19, 2026·9 min read

The short answer

Three reasons your apps matter more now

Copilot lives inside them

Users can ask questions about the data in your canvas and model-driven apps without leaving the app. You switch it on, no rebuild.

They handle more data

The data row limit for queries that can't be delegated now goes up to 10,000 records, which gives you a lot more headroom.

Agents can use them

Your Dataverse data, business rules and apps become context and tools that Copilot, Cowork, Copilot Studio and coding agents can work with, under your existing permissions.

My take

Think of your Power Apps as the company's filing system and front desk. Agents are the new staff. They don't replace the filing system. They need it, and the better organized it is, the better they work.

Copilot in your apps

Your users get a Copilot button

  • GA

    Microsoft Copilot in model-driven apps. Users ask questions and get answers grounded in the records on screen and across their business and productivity data.

  • Preview

    Microsoft Copilot in canvas apps. Users select Copilot in the upper-right corner and ask about the app's Dataverse or SharePoint data in plain language. Microsoft said at Ignite it's heading to GA. Check your tenant.

  • GA

    App skills for data entry, exploration, visualization and summarization.

  • GA

    Agent feed with the Power Apps MCP Server. A place inside your app where users supervise what agents did, with human oversight built in.

  • GA

    App-based forms and grids in Copilot Chat, so parts of your model-driven app show up where people already work. Custom UX for this is in preview.

Why it matters

The hardest part of most business apps is getting people to find what they need. A plain-language question beats five filters and a gallery search.

Licensing

Copilot in model-driven apps needs both a Power Apps Premium license and a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Copilot in canvas apps needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot license.

Building apps

Building got a lot faster

  • Preview

    Authoring agent in canvas studio. Build and edit canvas apps by asking, right inside Power Apps Studio. It works with existing apps too, and runs through your GitHub account.

  • GA

    Canvas authoring plugin for coding agents. Build canvas apps from GitHub Copilot CLI or Claude Code instead of the studio.

  • GA

    Model app-builder skill, the same agent-assisted approach for whole model-driven apps.

  • GA

    Generative pages: describe a page and get it built with Fluent UI controls.

  • Available

    Data row limit up to 10,000. Raise it in Settings › General › Data row limit.

  • Also new

    The modern Card control (preview), the modern Data Grid control, user-defined types (GA), and continued quality fixes for modern controls.

Don't skip this

A bigger row limit isn't a substitute for delegation. If your data source has more records than the limit and your formula isn't delegable, you still get partial results, with no error.

My testing trick, straight from the docs

While you build, set the data row limit to 1. Anything that isn't delegated returns a single record, so you spot the problem in testing instead of in production.

Your apps as agent tools

Your apps and data get a second job

This is the big one. Business Applications in Work IQ connects Microsoft's work context layer to the business data in Dynamics 365 and Power Apps. Your apps stop being somewhere people go, and become something every AI tool can use.

  • GA

    Work IQ context and actions, now including your business apps data.

  • Preview

    Business apps in Cowork: ask "Which opportunities are at risk?" across your Dataverse and custom Power Apps tables without knowing table names.

  • Preview

    Business apps in Copilot Studio: add your Dynamics 365 and Power Apps context to agents through the Work IQ tool.

  • Available

    Work IQ MCP, Dataverse MCP and the Dataverse plugin for coding agents, so coding agents can reason over your business data and build on it.

Why it matters

Ten years of Dataverse tables, security roles and business rules aren't technical debt anymore. They're the context that makes agents useful. Your permissions still apply, so an agent only sees what the person using it can see.

Admins, one setting changed

The Dataverse intelligence (preview) setting is now the Work IQ setting. Turn it on in the Power Platform admin center to keep using Dataverse data with Copilot and agents.

For pro devs

Code apps keep getting better

Power Apps code apps let you build in React, Vue or another framework from VS Code, with Entra sign-in, 1,500+ connectors callable from JavaScript, and your managed platform policies. Code apps now have alerting and metrics in Power Platform monitor.

If that sounds a lot like Copilot Managed Runtime apps, you're right. They overlap. The difference is mostly licensing and where they're governed. The picker walks through it →

Licensing

Users running a code app need Power Apps Premium, pay-as-you-go, an App Pass, or a Dynamics 365 license that includes Power Apps use rights. An admin also has to turn on code apps for the environment.

Do this week

Five things to do with your existing apps

  1. Find apps using the old Copilot control

    Since February 2, 2026 you can't add the old Copilot control or a custom Copilot to new canvas apps, and existing ones will eventually stop being supported. Plan the move to Microsoft Copilot in canvas apps.

  2. Turn Copilot on for one pilot app

    Pick a model-driven app your users already like, turn on Microsoft Copilot in model-driven apps in Settings › Features, publish, and watch what people ask.

  3. Check your row limits

    Open your biggest canvas apps, look for delegation warnings, and raise the data row limit only where it helps.

  4. Ask your admin about the Work IQ setting

    It's what lets Copilot, Cowork and agents reach your Dataverse data.

  5. Clean up one table's descriptions

    Agents read table and column names to understand your data. "cr4f2_status2" doesn't help anyone. Clear names and descriptions do.

Why start small

One pilot app tells you what users actually ask Copilot, which shows you what to fix in your data first. That beats turning it on everywhere and hoping.

Gotchas

Copilot in canvas apps, current limits

  • Your app can use SharePoint or Dataverse, not both in the same app.
  • For SharePoint, only apps with a single SharePoint list return answers during the preview.
  • Lists connected through an environment variable aren't read.
  • It's read-only. To change data, you customize it with an agent.
  • It's not available in the Power Apps mobile app yet.
  • With Dataverse, it relies on Dataverse Search indexes, so make sure search is on and your tables are indexed.

Bottom line

Model-driven apps on Dataverse get the best Copilot experience today. Canvas apps on one SharePoint list work, with limits. Plan pilots around that.
