PART 1 · FOR MAKERS · THE COMMON THREAD

Agents are being set up like coworkers

If you only remember one thing from this Ignite, make it this. Agents are getting the same things we give a new hire.

Identity

Their own account, so actions are traceable.

Memory

They carry context between tasks.

Triggers

They can start work when something happens, not only when asked.

Governance

Admins can see, limit and pay for them like any other resource.

Copilot Studio

Agents, workflows and apps in one place

  • GA

    Apps in Copilot Studio and Copilot Cowork, rolling out this month.

  • Public preview

    Build Copilot Studio agents and workflows from Cowork or Copilot Code, in natural language.

Why it matters

Most processes need judgment, fixed steps, and a screen where people add information. With all three in one studio, you design around the process, not the tool.

Agent identity

Agents get their own accounts

  • Preview
    • Dedicated agent accounts, starting December 2026
    • Memory
    • Signal-based triggers

Why it matters

When an agent has its own account, you can @mention it, hand it a task, and review every action it took. That's what makes it auditable, not just helpful.

Build together

Agents built by a team, not one person

  • GA

    Agent sharing for multiple makers.

  • Public preview
    • Copilot Studio CLI and Copilot Studio MCP
    • Local editing with GitHub-backed artifacts

Why it matters

Single-owner agents tend to stall after the pilot. Co-authoring and source control let a team own, review and roll back an agent over time.

Customer-facing agents

Handing off to a person, with context

  • GA

    Customer conversation harness for voice and chat, with live-agent handoff.

Customer result shared at Ignite

Asurion: touchless invoice processing went from under 20% to over 70% with a Copilot Studio agent.

Why it matters

When the agent hands off with context, the customer doesn't have to start over with a human.

Copilot Cowork

Cowork picks the effort for the task

  • Frontier

    Auto with subagents, plus proactive recommendations.

  • GA

    Brand Kit, app building, and Cowork in Edge for Business.

Why it matters

Auto sets reasoning effort per task. Quick lookups stay fast, and bigger jobs split across subagents running side by side. You can still set the effort yourself.

Microsoft reports nearly 80% of the Fortune 500 now use Cowork.

Power Platform

You don't have to start over

  • GA
    • Copilot in canvas apps, this November
    • Canvas delegation limit raised to 10,000 items
    • Business Applications in Work IQ: existing apps, flows and sites become tools agents can use
Example from Ignite

G&J Pepsi is bringing agents into Power Platform solutions it has run for a decade, without rebuilding them.

Why it matters

Agents can work on the apps and flows you've already built, not just new ones. Copilot in canvas apps respects the app's existing data access and permissions.

Cost & value

Know the cost before you scale

  • GA
    • Credit usage visibility in Copilot Studio
    • Agent 365 Cost Management: spend limits, alerts and model choice
  • Frontier

    Agent 365 Business Analytics: measures work completed, not just tokens.

Why it matters

After a good pilot, the next question is always "what will this cost at scale?" These give makers and admins that answer before the invoice does.

PART 2 · COPILOT FOR EVERYONE

The Copilot news everyone's talking about

Part 1 was for people who build. These are the announcements your whole company will notice, and the ones your leadership is going to ask you about.

Copilot Workspaces

Copilot goes multiplayer

  • Frontier

    Workspaces replace Copilot Notebooks. Your team, your files and your agents share one space and one set of context. Agents draft, you review, and a comment on a doc is enough to get an agent working.

Read my Workspaces team playbook →

Why it matters

Until now, Copilot's context lived in one person's chat. Workspaces put it where the whole team can use it, so nobody has to re-explain the project.

Autopilot

An agent that keeps working when you don't

  • Frontier
    • Always on: watches Teams channels, follows up on emails, runs recurring work and manages projects without waiting for a prompt
    • Handles one-off requests and routines, like a weekly summary sent every Sunday
    • Its own Microsoft Entra Agent ID and a named manager

Why it matters

You decide what it takes on, and it checks with you before key actions. IT can scope its access and block it anytime. That's the bar for handing off real work: it acts on its own, but nothing important goes out without a person saying yes.

Plugins

Copilot learns your job

  • GA

    Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service plugins for Copilot.

  • Available

    New role plugins for sales, service, finance and legal. Microsoft lists 44 sales, 13 customer service, 30 finance and 20+ legal skills.

What's in a plugin

Skills (the how-to), connectors (approved access to data) and agents, packaged together. Admins approve, assign and govern them from one plugin registry.

Why it matters

Instead of every person writing their own sales or contract-review prompts, your team's playbook ships once as a plugin and everyone starts from the same approved workflow.

Today

No more blank chat box

  • Private preview

    A command center in the Copilot app and Outlook across mail, calendar, Teams threads, meetings and tasks. It shows what you missed, what needs attention now and what can wait, with drafts and follow-ups ready to send.

Why it matters

The hardest part of using Copilot has always been knowing what to ask. Today flips that: Copilot opens with a suggestion and you decide.

Microsoft IQ

Your company's know-how, in one place for agents

  • Announced

    Microsoft IQ brings four layers together: Work IQ (how people collaborate, including past decisions), Fabric IQ (business data and rules), Foundry IQ (policies and trusted documents) and Web IQ (outside context), plus 350+ connectors.

  • Preview
    • Work IQ Memory, so agents remember what matters
    • Agent policy governance that pauses agents for human approval before consequential actions

Why it matters

Agents get better when they know how your company actually works, not just what's in a file. The flip side is governance: decide now what agents should and shouldn't learn from, and who reviews it.

The surprise · Copilotforce

Salesforce is coming to Copilot

  • Public preview
    • Salesforce in Copilot: a plugin with 38+ prebuilt sales skills, like pipeline reviews, deal planning, customer health and renewals
    • Data 360 to Fabric (OneLake) zero copy sharing
  • GA

    Zero copy data federation from OneLake into Salesforce Data 360.

  • Pilot

    Sales Cloud in Teams, with an open beta planned.

Good to know

It works within each seller's existing Salesforce permissions, and CRM updates need the user to confirm before they're saved.

Why it matters

Plenty of Microsoft 365 shops run sales on Salesforce. This means sellers can review pipeline and prep for calls in Copilot, with their email, meetings and docs, without switching apps, and without copying CRM data into another system.
