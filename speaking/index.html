About April

I’m April Dunnam, a keynote speaker, author, and co-founder of Agent Academy. I started as a .NET and SharePoint developer and implemented Microsoft 365 solutions for clients before I began teaching Power Platform in 2017. Today, 55K people subscribe to my YouTube tutorials. I’m also the author of The Copilot Compass.
